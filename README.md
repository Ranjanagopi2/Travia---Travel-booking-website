Trivia – Empowering Personalized Travel Planning

In today’s fast-paced world, travel planning can often feel overwhelming. From choosing destinations and managing budgets to organizing day-wise activities, travelers juggle multiple tools and spreadsheets just to plan a single trip.
That’s where Trivia – Empowering Personalized Travel Planning comes in.

🌍 Vision

Trivia envisions becoming an intelligent, personalized, and collaborative platform that transforms how individuals plan and experience travel.
The goal is simple:
To make travel planning as exciting as the journey itself.
By combining structured itineraries, budget insights, interactive timelines, and community sharing, Trivia enables users to dream, design, and organize trips effortlessly.

🎯 Mission

Our mission is to build a user-centric and responsive travel planning application that simplifies multi-city trip management.

The platform empowers travelers to:
Create customized multi-city itineraries
Assign travel dates, activities, and budgets
Discover destinations through search
Visualize their trips through calendars and timelines
Share travel plans publicly or with friends

At its core, the application is powered by a well-structured relational database that handles complex travel data such as itineraries, stops, activities, and cost breakdowns.

🚀 Key Features
1️⃣ Secure Login & Signup
Personalized accounts with authentication and profile management.

2️⃣ Interactive Dashboard
A centralized hub to view upcoming trips, recommended destinations, and budget highlights.

3️⃣ Smart Itinerary Builder
Add cities, assign travel dates, organize day-wise activities, and reorder stops seamlessly.

4️⃣ City & Activity Search
Discover cities with cost indexes and popularity insights.
Explore activities filtered by type, cost, and duration.

5️⃣ Automated Budget Estimation
Detailed cost breakdowns by transport, stay, meals, and activities — with visual charts and alerts for overspending.

6️⃣ Calendar & Timeline Visualization
Interactive calendar view and structured timeline layout to visualize the full journey.

7️⃣ Shareable Public Itineraries
Generate public links for trips and allow others to view, get inspired, or copy the plan.

8️⃣ User Profile & Preferences
Manage profile settings, language preferences, saved destinations, and privacy controls.

9️⃣ Admin & Analytics Dashboard (Optional)
Track popular cities, user engagement, and overall platform growth.

💡 Technical Perspective

Trivia demonstrates:
Proper implementation of relational database design
Dynamic user interfaces adapting to each trip’s flow
Real-time cost calculations
Structured storage and retrieval of complex travel data

The focus was not just on functionality, but also on creating an intuitive and visually engaging user experience.

🌟 Why Trivia Matters

Travel is deeply personal. Every traveler has unique preferences, budgets, and dreams. Trivia aims to provide the flexibility and intelligence required to turn those dreams into well-structured, actionable travel plans.
Because planning your trip should feel just as exciting as taking it.
